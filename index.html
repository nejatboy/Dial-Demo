Dial demo playlist

A playlist for trying Dial, the media player for your own playlists. Dial doesn't provide any content; everything here is free to share: public domain works (including works of the U.S. government such as NASA, NOAA, NPS and USGS), CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA. Videos are re-encoded (and channel clips joined into loops); titles shown in the app are ours, the original titles are on each source page.

M3U: https://nejatboy.github.io/Dial-Demo/dial.m3u
