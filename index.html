Dial demo playlist

A small M3U playlist to try the Dial player. Paste this link into Dial's "Add playlist" screen:

https://nejatboy.github.io/Dial-Demo/dial.m3u

Live channels are generated test patterns with a TV guide (XMLTV). Movies are short excerpts of Blender open movies. Nothing here is real broadcast content.
